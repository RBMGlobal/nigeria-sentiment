New national evidence: Yiaga NVIS 3.0

Presidential performance: 22% approve, 54% disapprove, 24% indifferent. Separately, 61% say fear of violence could prevent them voting. These are national survey results, not state scores or a measure of NSA/ONSA approval.

Dates and sample: published 7 October 2026; fieldwork September 2026 (exact days not disclosed). Yiaga Africa commissioned ISCL/API: 2,470 adults aged 18+, covering 36 states and FCT; 2,066 telephone interviews and 404 rural face-to-face interviews.

Limits: telephone sampling used random dialling and panel contacts; rural respondents were convenience-selected at six sites. Results were weighted by sex within zone, not age. Youth are underrepresented. The stated ±2.1-point national margin is only a nominal guide for this mixed sample. Added rural interviews and differing measures prevent a like-for-like trend comparison with the older dashboard polls. No national result has been assigned to states.

Primary report: methods pp. 14–17; findings pp. 21, 23 · Reviewed 12 October 2026.
